## 👋 About me

I'm a frontend-focused full-stack developer from Colombo, Sri Lanka. I've been building for the web since school and have spent about seven years shipping real products for clients.

- 🏢 **Mar–Sep 2026:** Developer at **Sri Lanka Telecom**, where I built and led production React / Next.js platforms used across SLT and SLTMobitel
- 🏆 Two of those platforms won **Merit Awards at the National ICT Awards**
- 🎓 Final-year **BSc (Hons) IT, Data Science** at SLIIT. Research: explainable multi-label AI for Alzheimer's and Parkinson's
- 🎨 What I enjoy most: turning a mockup or screenshot into a clean, working UI, then hunting down the bugs nobody else could reproduce
- 🟢 Open to frontend / full-stack roles and remote contract work
