## 👋 Hey, I'm Rajitha

I started building websites back in grade 10. That turned into about seven years of freelance work for local businesses, and somewhere along the way I realised the part I enjoy most is the frontend: taking a rough idea or a screenshot and turning it into something people actually like using.

This year I spent six months (Mar–Sep 2026) as a developer at **Sri Lanka Telecom**, working on internal platforms used across SLT and SLTMobitel. Two of the projects I worked on there went on to win **Merit Awards at the National ICT Awards**.

Right now I'm in my final year of a Data Science degree at **SLIIT**. My research team is building explainable AI for Alzheimer's and Parkinson's diagnosis, so most days I'm switching between React components and PyTorch notebooks.

I'm open to frontend and full-stack roles, and remote contract work. If you're building something interesting, I'd love to hear about it.
